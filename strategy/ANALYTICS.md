# ANALYTICS

Measure what the strategy claims to care about. If community is the first
objective, community has to be the first thing measured — otherwise revenue
metrics quietly become the goal because they're the ones on the dashboard.

## 1. Tier 1 — Community (reviewed weekly)

| Metric | Definition | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Members** | THE ROOTS registrations, consented | The first objective |
| **Contribution rate** | % of members who contributed (research, correction, translation, photo, vote) in 90 days | A member who never participates is a subscriber |
| **Ritual attendance** | Attendees / members, per ritual | Whether rituals are real |
| **Correction rate** | Corrections raised by members per chapter | Proof the archive is being read critically — **a rising number is good** |
| **Word of mouth** | % of new members citing a person as source | The only distribution that compounds |
| **UGC** | Member-created pieces per chapter, with consent | Culture, not campaign |

## 2. Tier 2 — Brand

Direct traffic share · branded search volume ("MAJDAL", "Majdal hoodie",
"Majdalawi streetwear") · archive depth (entries read per session) · return
visitor rate · archive→member conversion.

**Archive depth is the key leading indicator.** If people read one entry and
leave, the research is decoration. If they read three, the brand is working.

## 3. Tier 3 — Commercial

Conversion rate · sell-through per chapter · AOV · repeat purchase rate ·
CAC (blended and paid) · ROAS (only for amplification of proven creative) ·
return rate (and reason codes — sizing errors are a product fix) ·
**contribution margin after the 15% impact commitment**, which is the number
that tells us whether the business is actually viable *with* the commitment.

## 4. Tier 4 — Impact

Committed · transferred · confirmed · outstanding · **days from accrual to
transfer** (target ≤90, published when missed) · recipients verified.

Sourced from `impact/ledger.json` only. There is no second set of numbers.

## 5. Implementation

**Currently: no analytics are installed.** That is deliberate — we are not
adding a tracker before there is a privacy policy and a consent mechanism.

When added:
- Privacy-first, cookieless, EU-hosted, no cross-site tracking, no data sale.
- Consent before any non-essential measurement, and the site works fully
  without consent.
- Never track members individually to target them.
- Server-side conversion only where it does not require profiling.

Collecting behavioural data on a community built around Palestinian history,
then handing it to an ad platform, would contradict the entire brand. The
measurement stack is a values decision, not a tooling decision.

## 6. Review cadence

Weekly: Tier 1. Per chapter: all tiers, published in the chapter report where
Tier 4 is concerned. Quarterly: does the data contradict the strategy?

**The honest question at every review:** are we measuring community because we
believe in it, or reporting revenue because it's easier? If the last three
decisions all optimised commercial metrics, the strategy has drifted.
