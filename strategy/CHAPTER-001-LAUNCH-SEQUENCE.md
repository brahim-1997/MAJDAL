# CHAPTER 001 — ROOTS · LAUNCH SEQUENCE

A 12-week sequence. The shape: **research in public → community → product last.**
Most brands invert this and start with the product reveal. Inverting it is what
makes MAJDAL's first chapter credible rather than another launch.

**Precondition — nothing in weeks 1–12 ships until the P0 cultural reviews in
`cultural-research/REVIEWS.md` are answered.** That is a hard gate, not a
scheduling preference.

## Phase 1 — THE RECORD (weeks 1–4)
*No product exists publicly. No logo reveal. No countdown.*

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Publish `/story` and archive entries A001–A003. One sentence of context, no brand push. Direct-invite the first 50 to THE ROOTS, individually written. |
| 2 | A004–A005. First **THE READING** (A001 — *Two thousand looms*). Paid researcher guest if secured. |
| 3 | A006–A008. Short film 01: *Eight metres* — a hand, a loom, thread, no product, no voiceover, text only. |
| 4 | Publish the impact ledger at zero with the methodology. Publishing a zero ledger before launch is the single most differentiating move available and costs nothing. |

**Success at week 4** is not follower count. It is: 50+ members who can state
what MAJDAL is in their own words, and at least one correction from a member.

## Phase 2 — THE CRAFT (weeks 5–8)
*Garments appear as objects, still not as products.*

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Fabric, not clothes: 480 GSM loopback, cut open, weighed on camera. The named-cloths system explained — `ikhdari`, `jiljileh`, `malak`. |
| 6 | Colour-block construction shown as loom logic: woven band → panel seam. This is the brand's design argument; make it once, properly. |
| 7 | **THE TABLE**, city one. Members only, free, small. Photograph the room, not the merchandise. |
| 8 | Product photography drops as editorial, not catalogue. Prices published. No "link in bio" energy. |

## Phase 3 — THE CHAPTER (weeks 9–12)

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 9 | Full chapter page live: the 480, the four garments, every colourway sourced. THE ROOTS vote opens on the chapter's impact recipient. |
| 10 | **THE ROOTS access window opens — 72 hours.** Long enough to think. Vote result published with counts. |
| 11 | Public open. State plainly what remains. Never invent "almost gone". |
| 12 | **CHAPTER CLOSE.** Publish units, eligible revenue, committed, transferred, outstanding, recipient. Same day, every time. |

## Post-chapter (weeks 13+)

Post-drop storytelling is where most brands go silent and lose everything they
built. Ours: garments photographed on members in their cities, by name and
consent; the first impact transfer documented end-to-end with receipt; the
Chapter 002 subject announced as a research question, not a teaser.

## The launch rules

1. **No countdown to an unconfirmed date.** If production slips, say so.
2. **No fake scarcity.** 480 is the real PO. If it changes, the number changes.
3. **No sales post before week 8.** Four weeks of record, four of craft.
4. **No Palestinian suffering imagery. Ever.**
5. **If a P0 review is unanswered at week 9, the chapter slips.** Cultural
   accuracy outranks the calendar, and a slipped chapter is survivable in a way
   a printed error is not.
