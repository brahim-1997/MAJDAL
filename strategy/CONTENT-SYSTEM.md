# CONTENT SYSTEM

Every product generates a complete content ecosystem. This document is the
template so it is produced deliberately rather than improvised after shipping.

## 1. The per-product ecosystem

For each of the four Chapter 001 garments, produce:

| # | Asset | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Teaser** (still) | Fabric or construction detail. Never the full garment first. |
| 2 | **Editorial set** (6–10 stills) | Real bodies, hard light, plain ground, tight crops. |
| 3 | **Short film** (20–40s) | One idea. No music-video montage. |
| 4 | **Archive story** | The documented fact the garment references, with sources. |
| 5 | **Construction breakdown** | Why the panels sit where they sit. |
| 6 | **Community content** | The garment worn by named members, with consent. |
| 7 | **Post-drop story** | Two weeks after close: the garment in use, not on a rail. |

**Ratio rule: no more than 1 in 4 posts is a sales post.** Enforce it by
counting monthly, not by feel.

## 2. Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **THE RECORD** | 40% | Archive entries, sources, corrections. The research *is* the marketing. |
| **THE CRAFT** | 25% | Fabric, construction, weight, seams, loom logic. |
| **THE ROOTS** | 20% | Rituals, members, contributions, votes, chapter reports. |
| **THE PRODUCT** | 15% | Actual selling. The smallest pillar, on purpose. |

If THE PRODUCT exceeds 15% in a month, the community is being farmed and the
next month must correct it.

## 3. Four-week rolling calendar

| | Mon | Wed | Fri | Sun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **W1** | Archive entry | Craft detail | Member/ritual | Long-form archive |
| **W2** | Archive entry | Construction | THE READING recap | Short film |
| **W3** | Archive entry | Fabric/spec | Community vote or Q&A | Product editorial |
| **W4** | Archive entry | Craft detail | Chapter/impact numbers | Correction & sources |

The Sunday W4 slot is permanent: corrections, source updates, open questions.
A brand that publishes its own corrections on a schedule is doing something
almost nobody does, and it is the strongest possible proof the research is real.

## 4. Campaign film — Chapter 001

**Title:** EIGHT METRES · **Length:** 90s · **No voiceover. Text only.**

| Beat | Image | Text on screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Thread on a spool, macro, slow | *Al-Majdal. A town on the coast.* |
| 2 | Hands, loom treadle, rhythm | *Two thousand looms. Most of them inside houses.* |
| 3 | Cloth advancing, stripe forming | *Eight metres made one dress.* |
| 4 | Same shot, held uncomfortably long | *One to two months of work.* |
| 5 | Cut to black. Hold 2s | *1948.* |
| 6 | Empty loom, static | *Eleven thousand people. Expelled in stages.* |
| 7 | Hands weaving again — different room, present day | *The weavers kept weaving.* |
| 8 | Cloth, close | *The cloth kept the name. Majdalawi.* |
| 9 | Garment detail: panel seam where the band sits | *MAJDAL. Chapter 001 — ROOTS.* |
| 10 | Black | *48* |

**Rules:** no archival footage without provenance and permission. No suffering
imagery. If we cannot secure real loom footage ethically and with payment, we
shoot the garment and the text only — a lesser film is better than an extracted
one.

## 5. Motion principles

Entrances only. 400–600ms. Nothing bounces, nothing spins, no parallax on text,
no scroll-jacking. If motion is the most interesting thing on screen, the work
underneath wasn't strong enough.

## 6. Photography

Archival material is presented as **document** — full frame, visible grain,
captioned with source and date, never filtered to fake age. Product is hard
light, plain ground, real bodies, tight crops. Every archival image needs
provenance before publication. No provenance, no publication.
