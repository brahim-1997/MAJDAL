# THE ROOTS — COMMUNITY STRATEGY

**Objective: build a real community. Not maximise first-chapter revenue.**

That ordering is the strategy. It is also expensive, so it has to be written
down, or the first slow sales week will quietly reverse it.

## 1. Why community before sales, concretely

A chapter-based brand with no community is a brand that must buy attention for
every release, forever. Paid acquisition cost rises, the work gets shaped by
what performs, and the cultural subject becomes content. Community is the only
asset that compounds: it gives early demand without discounting, correction
before errors reach production, and distribution that isn't rented.

For MAJDAL there is a second reason. We are handling other people's history.
A brand that answers only to its customers will eventually round that history
off toward whatever sells. A brand that answers to a community that includes
Palestinians will not get away with it — which is the point.

## 2. Membership

Free. No spend tier, no points, no status from order history. A member who
has never bought anything and a member who has bought every chapter have the
same standing and the same vote.

**What a member actually gets:**

1. **The archive first** — every entry, with sources, before publication.
   Members are our error-correction layer.
2. **The access window** — chapters open to THE ROOTS first, long enough to
   think. Not a sixty-second scramble engineered to cause panic.
3. **One real decision per chapter** — a colourway, a garment, or which
   verified recipient the chapter's impact goes to. We publish the count and
   we honour it, including when we'd have chosen differently.
4. **Named contribution** — research, translation, photography, family records.
   Credited by name unless they ask otherwise. Paid when it is real work.

## 3. Rituals

Things that happen on a schedule whether or not there is something to sell.

| Ritual | Cadence | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **THE READING** | Monthly | One archive entry, read and discussed live. A researcher or practitioner joins when the subject calls for it — **paid**. |
| **THE TABLE** | Per chapter, in person | A shared meal in one city. Free, small, cameras are not the point. |
| **CHAPTER CLOSE** | End of chapter | The numbers, in public, on the day the chapter closes. Every time, including bad ones. |
| **THE HANDOVER** | Annual | Members bring one object, photograph or story their family carried — only with the permission of whoever it belongs to. |

THE READING is the load-bearing one: it is cheap, it is monthly, it creates a
reason to belong that has nothing to do with buying, and it directly serves
the research the brand depends on.

## 4. Growth — in order

1. **Fifty people who actually care.** Direct, personal invitations. No ads.
   Founder writes to each one. This stage cannot be skipped or bought.
2. **The archive as the front door.** The research is the marketing. People
   arrive for a documented story about a weaving town, not for a hoodie.
3. **Word of mouth with something to carry.** Every member has one thing they
   can say in a sentence: *a town that made cloth, erased, and the cloth kept
   its name.* Give people a true sentence and they will repeat it.
4. **In person.** THE TABLE in one city per chapter. Physical presence is what
   separates a community from an audience.
5. **Paid, last, and only to amplify what already worked organically.**

## 5. Rules

- Never sell or share member data.
- Never make every message a sales message. Target: no more than 1 in 4.
- Never use the community as a marketing asset in front of the community —
  no "look how engaged our community is" content aimed at the community.
- Never run a countdown to a date we have not committed to.
- Never use images of Palestinian suffering to move product.
- When we get something wrong, correct it at the same volume, in public.

## 6. Measurement

See `strategy/ANALYTICS.md`. The community metrics that matter are
contribution rate, ritual attendance, and correction rate — not follower count.
Followers are not members.
