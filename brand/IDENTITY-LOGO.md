# IDENTITY — THE MARK

Audit of the supplied logo sheet, the system built from it, and four errors
that must be fixed before anything is printed.

---

## 1. STOP — errors in the supplied artwork

### 1.1 The coordinates are wrong `CRITICAL`

The sheet carries **`32.3030° N 34.7875° E`**. That is not al-Majdal. It is
roughly 70 km north, in the Sharon plain near Netanya, and it matches none of
the six places listed on the same sheet.

| | Latitude | Longitude |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On the artwork | 32.3030° N | 34.7875° E |
| **Al-Majdal (Asqalan)** | **31.6749° N** | **34.5750° E** |

*Sources: Ashkelon coordinates 31.6748881 / 34.5750365; the railway station named
Majdal from 1917–1948 sits at 31°40′35″N 34°36′16″E.*

This is exactly the failure `cultural-research/al-majdal-dossier.md` §2 warned
about when it marked coordinates `UNVERIFIED FOR PRINT`. A brand whose entire
claim is *we do not invent, we source* cannot ship a garment with a number
pointing at the wrong place. It is also the kind of error that, once found by
someone who knows the geography, discredits every other claim we make.

**Also note:** even the corrected figure is the **modern city**, not a surveyed
fix on the historic town centre. Label it honestly — `31.67° N 34.58° E ·
approximate, modern city` — or omit the decimals entirely.

### 1.2 `®` is a legal risk `CRITICAL`

The wordmark carries **®**, which asserts a *registered* trademark. If MAJDAL
is not registered in the territory where the goods are sold, using ® is a false
claim of registration and is an offence in several jurisdictions, the UK
included.

**Use `™` until registration is granted.** The sheet already uses ™ on the
mark — make it consistent, and drop ® everywhere until a certificate exists.

### 1.3 `ROOPS` is a typo

Bottom-right label reads **`ROOPS`**. It should be **`ROOTS`**.

### 1.4 System conflicts

| On the sheet | Conflicts with | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DROP 001` | The brand releases **chapters**, not drops. "Drop" is the generic streetwear word the brand is trying not to use | Use `CHAPTER 001` |
| `001 / 100` | `BRAND-BOOK.md` §4 sets the run at **480**, the real purchase order. Rule: never invent quantities | Either the PO is 100 and every doc updates, or the label is wrong. **Decide from the PO, not from the design** |

## 2. What is strong — and why

**The tower.** The mark reads as an angular tower or mountain form. **Majdal
(مجدل) means "tower".** So the silhouette is not decoration — it is the name,
drawn. That is the single best thing on the sheet, it is culturally justified,
and it is defensible in a way no borrowed streetwear mark would be.

**"EVERY PLACE HAS A MEMORY."** Strong, and strategically load-bearing: it is a
promise the interactive map actually keeps. A tagline the product delivers on
is worth more than one that only describes a feeling.

**The metadata band.** Coordinates, `48`, the globe, the place list — this is
the archive-label register the design system already calls for. Correct the
numbers and this becomes the brand's most recognisable non-logo asset.

**The place list.** `MAJDAL · YAFA · NABLUS · JERUSALEM · HAIFA · AKKA` is a
better first cohort for the map than the 418 depopulated villages: major
Palestinian cities, far better documented, far less ethically fraught as a
commercial brand's first cartographic act. Good instinct.

## 3. The olive leaves — a rule to change deliberately

`brand/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` §7 currently bans an olive pictogram: *"an olive tree
logo is the single most predictable move available to this brand and it is
therefore banned."* The supplied mark includes olive leaves.

**That rule was written to prevent the lazy version, and it should now be
narrowed rather than enforced blindly.** The distinction that matters:

| Banned | Permitted |
| --- | --- |
| An olive **tree** as the logo — the predictable move | Olive **leaves as a secondary element** beside a form that means something else |
| Olive as the brand's whole idea | Olive as one term in a compound: **tower + growth + star** |

The mark survives because the **tower carries the meaning** and the olive
modifies it. If the tower were removed and the olive remained, it would fail.

**Test to apply to any future application:** cover the olive leaves. Is it
still recognisably MAJDAL? On this mark, yes — the tower silhouette holds. That
is the condition of the exemption, and it is now the rule.

**Still banned:** olive as a repeating background pattern, olive wreaths, an
olive tree illustration as a hero image, olive + red/green as a colour scheme.

## 4. The lockups

| Lockup | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary stacked** — mark / MAJDAL / مجدل / coordinates / tagline | Homepage overture, campaign openers, one per surface |
| **Horizontal** — mark + MAJDAL + مجدل | Site header, garment labels, small sizes |
| **Stamp** — circular, mark centred | Packaging, woven labels, stickers |
| **`48` over مجدل** | The quiet mark. Inner neck, size tab, box print |
| **Wordmark alone** | Anywhere the mark would be under ~24px |

**Clear space:** the height of the `M` on all sides.
**Minimum sizes:** mark 16px; horizontal lockup 100px wide; stamp 32px.
**Never:** stretch, rotate, recolour outside the palette, add effects, place the
Arabic as ornament behind the Latin, or set the Arabic smaller than optical
parity with the Latin.

## 5. THE RULE OF THREE

The brand already organises in threes. Making it explicit turns a coincidence
into a system — and gives every designer and writer a structural default.

| Level | The three |
| --- | --- |
| **Story** | They carried it · We carry it · The next generation carries it forward |
| **Chapters** | ROOTS (cloth) · SABR (cactus) · BALADI (seed) |
| **Evidence** | VERIFIED · CONTESTED · INTERPRETATION |
| **UX depth** | Surface (30s) · Archive (5min) · Custody (returning) |
| **Type** | Display · Record · Reading — plus Arabic level with all three |
| **Colour roles** | Ground · Land · Signal |
| **Photography** | Documentary · Street · Editorial |
| **The mark** | Tower · Star · Olive |
| **Community** | Discover · Belong · Contribute |

**Applied rules:**
- Sections group in threes; a fourth item means the set is wrong.
- Copy lands in three beats. Two is abrupt, four dilutes.
- Three weights of information on any surface: **display, record, reading.**
- The map reveals in three depths: district → city → place.

**The exception that proves it:** the impact ledger has **four** states —
committed, transferred, confirmed, outstanding. Money does not bend to a
design principle. Where accuracy and the rule of three conflict, **accuracy
wins, every time.**

## 6. Before the mark is used anywhere

1. Fix the coordinates to `31.67° N 34.58° E`, labelled approximate — or remove.
2. Replace `®` with `™` until registration is granted.
3. Fix `ROOPS` → `ROOTS`.
4. `DROP 001` → `CHAPTER 001`.
5. Reconcile `001 / 100` against the real purchase order.
6. **Provenance for the archival photograph on the sheet.** It appears to show a
   town with a tower or minaret. Unattributed. Per `PROTOCOL.md`, no provenance
   means no publication — identify it, or do not use it.
7. Native-speaker review of مجدل, including whether to set it vocalised.

**Items 1, 2 and 6 are blocking.** They are a factual error, a legal exposure,
and an unsourced cultural image.
