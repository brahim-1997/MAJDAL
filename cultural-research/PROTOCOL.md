# CULTURAL RESEARCH PROTOCOL

MAJDAL is built on Palestinian memory. Memory is not a mood board.
Getting a fact wrong is not a typo — it damages the people whose history
we are working with, and it hands critics a reason to dismiss the brand.

This protocol is binding on every person and tool that produces MAJDAL
content: garments, captions, product copy, website text, campaign film,
interviews, decks.

## 1. The three tiers

Every cultural statement MAJDAL publishes must be classified before it ships.

| Tier | Meaning | May appear on product/campaign? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **`VERIFIED`** | Supported by at least two independent, citable sources, at least one of which is Palestinian or academic. | Yes — state plainly. |
| **`CONTESTED`** | Sources disagree, or the record is incomplete. | Only with the disagreement shown. Never flattened into a single confident claim. |
| **`INTERPRETATION`** | MAJDAL's own reading, design response, or feeling. | Yes — but must be unmistakably ours, never dressed as history. |

The failure mode we are guarding against is tier drift: an
`INTERPRETATION` that gets repeated until it is treated as `VERIFIED`.
Drift usually happens in captions and interviews, not in documents.

## 2. Language rules

- Write "documented" / "recorded" for `VERIFIED`. Write "accounts differ"
  for `CONTESTED`. Write "we read this as" / "for us" for `INTERPRETATION`.
- Never assign meaning to a tatreez motif, a colour, or a place unless a
  source assigns it. Decorative geometry is allowed; invented symbolism is not.
- Never present a MAJDAL garment as a traditional artefact. Our clothing is
  contemporary streetwear that references documented craft. It is not a thobe,
  it is not heritage wear, and it is not a reproduction.
- Use Palestinian place names first. Where a site is now known by another
  name, say so factually — that is part of the documented record.
- Arabic must be reviewed by a native speaker before it is printed, screen-
  printed, embroidered, or set as a logo. Machine-transliterated Arabic has
  shipped on other brands' garments with errors; it will not ship on ours.

## 3. The review gate

A cultural claim cannot reach production on our own authority alone.

**Gate 1 — Internal.** Claim written into `cultural-research/`, tiered, sourced.
**Gate 2 — Named external review.** A Palestinian historian, researcher, or
craft practitioner reviews the dossier for anything appearing on a garment,
a campaign film, or a permanent website page. Record reviewer name, date,
scope, and their corrections in `cultural-research/REVIEWS.md`.
**Gate 3 — Sign-off.** No unreviewed cultural claim goes to print. If Gate 2
has not happened, the claim ships as `INTERPRETATION` in MAJDAL's own voice,
or it does not ship.

> **Current status: Gate 2 has NOT been completed for any dossier in this
> repository.** Everything here is Gate 1. Sources are cited so a reviewer
> can check our work quickly. Treat the dossiers as a research brief for
> that review, not as cleared copy.

## 4. Working with practitioners

When MAJDAL works with Palestinian artists, researchers, weavers, or
embroiderers:

- Pay at professional rates, agreed in writing, in advance of delivery.
- Credit by name, publicly, unless the person asks not to be named.
- The practitioner keeps the rights to their own craft and prior work.
  We license a specific application for a specific chapter.
- Never brief a practitioner to "make it look Palestinian". Brief the actual
  garment, the actual technique, the actual constraints.
- Do not approach weavers in Gaza as a content opportunity. Any collaboration
  there is a commercial and logistical commitment with real money attached, or
  it does not happen.

## 5. When we get it wrong

We will get something wrong eventually. The response is fixed in advance:
correct it in public, at the same volume as the original claim, name what was
wrong, say what the record actually says, and log it in `REVIEWS.md`. No quiet
edits, no deleted posts.
