## Sources deliberately NOT used as cultural authority

- **ADISH SS21 / Majdalawi weaving** (surfaced in search). ADISH is an existing
  brand that has worked with Majdalawi weaving. Logged here so the team is aware
  of prior art and **does not converge on their execution**. We do not take
  cultural claims from a competitor's marketing, and we do not reference their
  campaign concepts, graphics or product treatments. Read it once, to know what
  not to do.
- Etsy / marketplace listings. Commercial copy, not a source.
- Advocacy organisations of any political alignment, for contested historical
  facts. Used only where they are the primary party (e.g. UNESCO's own record).
